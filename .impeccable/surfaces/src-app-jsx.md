## Scope
Whole team portal (all signed-in routes, plus sign-in and onboarding). Mode: Operate. Students on phones and staff on laptops, evenly.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Each event is presented like a science-fair tri-fold board: Prepare on the left panel, the event and its team in the middle, Results on the right. Refuses the SaaS default of greeting banner, stat-tile row, and soft rounded cards.
OWN-WORLD: Chantilly purple (#4b2a8a) board as the rail and fold backing; white mounted sheets framed by a purple gap and a thin black mat; one gold (#f2c230) title strip per event; Archivo Black uppercase cut-letter headings over Archivo body; square corners, no soft shadows, tabular figures for every number; status vocabulary fixed (not started / in progress / ready).
STORY: A student sees their events and next date immediately, opens an event to find what to study and how they're scoring. Staff see unstaffed events and unassigned members and fix them.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Purple rail left (logo, nav). Work area: uppercase page title with next-tournament line, then the tri-fold for the first of my events at full width, with Coming up and Announcements sheets below. Phone: top bar, the fold stacks as three sheets.
FORM: Science Fair Tri-fold, grounded list position 7. seed: none (unavailable). Raises: tabular data-dense numerals (Datamatics); fixed status vocabulary (Transit); one event owns the phone screen (Vertical Feed); strict 4px spacing module (Grid Specimen); nothing labeled twice (Catalog Sleeve).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints
No invented people, events, scores, or announcements anywhere; lists start empty. Keep logo, purple, and the name "Chantilly Science Olympiad".
